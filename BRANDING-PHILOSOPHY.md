# "Stadium Floodlight" — A Visual Philosophy

> Generated via the `canvas-design` skill as the conceptual soul of the brand.

## The Movement

**Stadium Floodlight** is the aesthetic of the moment just before kickoff: the roar held in
the lungs of eighty thousand people, the grass impossibly green under banks of white light,
the night sky pushed back to the rim of the bowl. It is celebration rendered with restraint —
festive energy disciplined into geometry. Joy, but engineered by a master hand.

## Space & Form

Darkness is the canvas, not the absence of one. A deep midnight base (`#0b1020`) lets every
element arrive like a player emerging from the tunnel into the light. Forms are bold, blocked,
and confident — large rounded panels float as if lit from within, separated by generous,
breathing negative space. Nothing is timid; nothing is crowded.

## Color & Material

Two pigments carry the whole composition: **championship gold** (`#FBBF24`) and **team red**
(`#DC2626`). Gold is the trophy, the medal, the decisive score — used sparingly so it always
reads as reward. Red is passion and motion. Against the night they glow rather than shout. The
material language is glass: translucent surfaces with a soft blur, edges catching a thin rim of
light, the painstaking sheen of something polished over countless hours.

## Scale & Rhythm

Numbers are heroes here — scores set large, tabular, and proud, isolated left-to-right inside
right-to-left text so the eye always trusts them. Hebrew display type (Assistant) provides
monumental headlines; Heebo carries the quieter passages. The rhythm is the rhythm of a match:
long builds of calm space, then a sudden bright accent — a CTA, a winning score — like a goal.

## Composition & Craft

Every alignment is deliberate, every gap measured. The work should feel meticulously crafted,
the product of deep expertise: the kind of interface a top studio would labor over, where the
glow of a button and the curve of a card are tuned until they feel inevitable. Master-level
execution, hidden inside something that simply feels like fun.

## The Quiet Reference

Woven invisibly into the palette and the three radial lights of the background: the **three host
nations of 2026** — the red, the gold, the green pitch — and the floodlit bowl of a stadium at
night. Those who know, feel it. Everyone else simply feels the celebration.
